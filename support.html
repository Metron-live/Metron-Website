Common questions

I can't sign in

If your phone number isn't receiving the SMS code, wait 60 seconds and request a new one. If it still doesn't arrive, your carrier may be filtering it — try again later, or email support@metron.live with the phone number you're using and we'll help directly.

If you signed up with Apple or Google, use the same provider you originally used. We don't merge accounts across providers.

Voice mode isn't working

Voice mode requires an Elite subscription and a microphone permission. Settings → Arete → Voice will show whether the permission was granted. On supported devices, speech recognition runs entirely on-device — your voice never leaves your phone.

Voice is rate-limited to 30 turns per day per user.

My HealthKit data isn't showing

Open the iOS Health app → Sharing → Apps → Metron, and confirm the data types you want shared are toggled on. If you've granted access but data still isn't visible, force-quit Metron and reopen — it re-reads HealthKit on launch.

Metron only reads the data types listed in our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy — workouts, energy, heart rate / HRV, sleep, body metrics, and a small set of fitness signals. We do not read respiratory rate, blood oxygen, ECG, or any clinical-record types.

Billing, subscriptions, refunds

Metron Elite is billed through Apple's subscription system. To view, change, or cancel your subscription: open the App Store app → tap your profile picture → Subscriptions → Metron. Cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing period.

For refunds, Apple handles all transactions. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. If Apple declines and you believe a refund is warranted, email support@metron.live and we'll advocate on your behalf.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Profile → Settings → Delete Account. The request is processed immediately and all personal data is removed from our servers within 30 days. You are signed out and returned to the welcome screen.

If you can't sign in to delete your account, email support@metron.live from the email address on the account.

What does Arete know about me?

Arete sees your full Metron profile — workouts, sleep, recovery signals, food logs, and any pillar data you've granted via HealthKit. It does not see anything outside Metron, and it cannot read your text messages, calendar, photos, or contacts.

Arete responses are routed to Anthropic's Claude model via our US-only infrastructure. Anthropic does not train on your data under our API agreement. See our Privacy Policy §2.3 for the full data flow.

Arete is giving advice that feels off

Arete is a wellness and fitness coaching companion — not a medical advisor. It does not diagnose conditions, recommend medications, or interpret lab results as medical findings. For anything medical, please consult a qualified healthcare provider.

If Arete makes a coaching suggestion that feels wrong for you, tell it — Arete adapts to your feedback. You can also email support@metron.live with the conversation context and we'll review.

Something else?

Email support@metron.live. Include your account email, device model (iPhone 14 Pro, etc.), and iOS version. We'll respond within one business day.
