The problem

Logging a workout is easy. Knowing what to do next isn't.

Most trackers preserve the numbers and stop there. Generic plans ignore the injury, the equipment you actually have, the practice calendar, and what happened in your last session.

Your history should make your next workout smarter. Metron turns the record of what you did into coaching for what you do next.

The product

The world's smartest personal trainer—built on a tracker good enough to stand alone.

Four connected loops take you from a real goal to the next better session.

01

Builds for your sport and goal

Metron shapes training around your goals, workout history, injuries, available equipment, and schedule. Endurance and sport-specific athletic preparation live inside Training.

02

Coaches and logs live

Move through the workout, record sets, and get timely guidance in one flow. Audio cues are designed to mix with—not take over—the music already playing.

03

Learns from every set

Each logged set strengthens your record. Review exercise history, weekly volume, workout streaks, and e1RM trends already backed by Metron's training and PR screens.

04

Adapts the next session

The coach uses performance plus verified recovery signals from Apple Health to adjust training. Every change remains visible, explainable, and yours to accept.
