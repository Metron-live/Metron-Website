# Metron Website Launch Messaging

## Positioning

- Primary promise: **Metron is the world's smartest personal trainer.**
- Free product: **the best free workout tracker.**
- Audience: **serious self-coached athletes.**
- Tracking, live logging, and workout history remain free.
- The full coach starts with a 30-day trial that requires no card. If the user
  does not continue, the free tracker remains.

## Product proof

1. **Train:** Builds sport- and goal-specific training around goals, history, injuries,
   equipment, and schedule.
2. **Log:** Coaches and logs live workouts without taking over the user's music.
3. **Learn:** Learns from logged sets and shows exercise history, weekly volume, workout
   streaks, and e1RM trends.
4. **Adapt:** Adapts training from performance and verified Apple Health recovery signals.

Arete chat and dedicated screens are two interfaces to the same plan, history,
and shared actions. Endurance and sport-specific athletic preparation belong
inside Training.

## Product horizon

Training is the only launch module. Injury mode, vacation mode,
technical-skill coaching, video form analysis, SmartRest, and a dedicated Watch
experience must always be labeled **Coming later**.

Do not publish testimonials, usage statistics, performance claims, pricing, or
integrations unless they have been independently verified and approved.

## Reservation

The reservation form is phone-first and also accepts email. The visitor verifies
a code before the page confirms eligibility. A founding reservation provides
the full coach free for the first year. This is distinct from the standard
launch offer: a 30-day full-coach trial with no card, followed by the free
tracker if the user does not continue. Use this confirmation:

> Welcome to the Metron family. Your free year is reserved. It's time to begin
> your journey to greatness.
