# Metron Website — Launch Messaging

## Positioning

- Primary promise: **Metron is the world's smartest personal trainer.**
- Free product: **a best-in-class workout tracker.**
- Tracking, live logging, and workout history remain free.
- The full coach starts with a 30-day trial that requires no card. If the user
  does not continue, the free tracker remains.

## Product proof

1. Builds sport- and goal-specific training around goals, history, injuries,
   equipment, and schedule.
2. Coaches and logs live workouts without taking over the user's music.
3. Learns from logged sets and shows exercise history, weekly volume, workout
   streaks, and e1RM trends.
4. Adapts training from performance and verified Apple Health recovery signals.

Arete chat and dedicated screens are two interfaces to the same plan, history,
and shared actions. Endurance and sport-specific athletic preparation belong
inside Training.

## Product horizon

Training is the launch foundation. The modular pillar architecture describes
future expansion, not a bundle of launch features. Injury mode, vacation mode,
technical-skill coaching, video form analysis, SmartRest, a dedicated Watch
experience, and broader pillars must always be labeled **Coming later**.

Do not publish testimonials, usage statistics, performance claims, pricing, or
integrations unless they have been independently verified and approved.

## Reservation

The reservation form is phone-first. A founding reservation provides the full
coach free for the first year. This is distinct from the standard launch offer:
a 30-day full-coach trial with no card, followed by the free tracker if the user
does not continue. Use this confirmation:

> Welcome to the Metron family. Your free year is reserved. It's time to begin
> your journey to greatness.
